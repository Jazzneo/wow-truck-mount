M2 Mount Workshop

Turn an .obj model into a World of Warcraft 3.3.5a mount: .m2 + 00.skin + .blp

1Drop your model

Drag files here

The .obj, its .mtl and the texture pictures (.png or .jpg). You can drop them all at once.

    Name the wheel parts “Wheel…” in your 3D program and they will spin when the mount moves.

    Lights: name parts “Lamp…” to make them glow at night, “…Glow” or “…Beam” for additive light effects, and “Brake…” for lights that only show while standing still.

    2Stand it up straight

    Wheels on the grid, nose toward the amber FRONT arrow. Each button turns it a quarter turn.

    3Seat and wheels

    Move the amber rider until they sit where the driver should be.

    4Save for WoW

    What is in the zip

    The model folder (Creature\…\Name.m2, Name00.skin, .blp textures) ready to copy into a patch MPQ, plus HOW_TO_INSTALL.txt with the DBC rows. Animations: Stand, Walk and Run. Rider points: mount (0), driver seat (39), passenger seat (40).
